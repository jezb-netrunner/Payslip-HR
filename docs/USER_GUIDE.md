# Payslip HR — Quick Guide

## Signing in
- **Admins** (company admins and client payroll/HR staff): **Admin** tab → email + password.
- **Employees**: **Employee** tab → the login code from HR (e.g. `ACME-7Q4KX9TB`, dash included).
- First-ever setup: the first account created becomes the company admin.

## Company admin — one-time setup
1. **Clients** → add each client (name, code, TIN, RDO, pay policy).
2. **Admin Users** → create entity admins and assign them their clients.
3. **Holidays** → keep national holidays current. **Statutory Tables** shows the tax and contribution tables payroll uses, read-only: they ship with the software (verified against BIR, SSS, PhilHealth and Pag-IBIG issuances) and can't be typed in, so a wrong rate can't slip in. When the dashboard says this year's check is due, or you hear of a new circular, ask the system maintainer to confirm the tables.

## Entity admin — pay cycle
Always pick the client first in the client selector at the top.

1. **Employees** → add employees (201 file, government IDs, pay rate, pay frequency) and generate each one's **login code**. Set their regular schedule on the employee's **Schedule** tab (**Change schedule** → the shift for each weekday, from a date on).
   **Pay rates**: change a rate on the employee's **Pay History** tab (or **Pay & Policy → Change pay rate**): the date it takes effect, the new rate, the reason and who approved it. Payroll pays each day at the rate in effect that day, so a raise from the 10th is prorated; a date ahead takes effect on its own. A rate dated back over payrolls already finalized doesn't change them: the tab lists them — reopen and recompute, or pay the difference as a one-time earning. A promotion recorded on **Career History** with a new rate lands in the pay history too. Employees don't see their pay history.
   **Fixed salary** (executives, managers, field staff, consultants on an employee retainer): on the **Pay & Policy** tab turn on *Fixed salary, no timekeeping*. The monthly rate pays each payroll — no time in/out needed, nothing docked for absence or lateness, no overtime or holiday premium; leave without pay is still deducted. *Gets 13th month pay* follows your company policy for them; service incentive leave follows the leave entitlements.
   **Output pay** (consultants or executives paid per deliverable, sales staff on commission, piece-rate workers): on **Pay & Policy** pick how *Output pay* is paid — *Paid by results* (the outputs are the basic pay) or fees, commission or other pay *on top of the salary* — and, for pay on top of the salary, whether it counts toward the 13th month (your company policy; it does by default). Then record each output on the employee's **Outputs** tab once accepted: the date, what was delivered, units × the rate or a fixed amount, who accepted it, and the output report (PDF, image, Word, Excel or CSV). The payroll covering the output's date pays it; a finalized payroll locks it (its report can still be attached). The payroll warns about outputs without a report and, for a worker paid by results, about a period that pays less than the minimum wage. An unworked regular holiday of a worker paid by results is paid the average daily earnings of the last 7 working days before it (their outputs and any rate), never less than the minimum wage — so record each output on the day it is accepted.
   **Independent consultants** (not employees) go on the **Consultants** page instead: add each with their TIN, registered address, contract, fee and the kind of fee (management/technical consultancy, or a licensed professional's fee). Before the first billing, answer the employee-or-contractor checklist for how the engagement really works and sign it off — if the client controls *how* the work is done, the person is an employee whatever the contract says. Record each payee's sworn declaration of gross income for the year (**Declarations**): without one, the higher rate applies. **Record billing** for each invoice: the system withholds the expanded withholding tax and shows the net to pay; **Mark paid** when paid. The **BIR (EWT)** tab has the quarter's 1601-EQ figures (and the 0619-E of its first two months), the alphalist of payees as CSV, the 2307 figures to print for each payee, the filing dates (**Change** one when the BIR extends it; **BIR & Reports → Due dates** lists them too) and the year's declarations list. After filing the 1601-EQ, **Mark filed**: the quarter's billings lock (reopen it to correct one).
   **Schedules** → **Shifts**: add the client's shifts once (e.g. Opening 6 AM–3 PM, Closing 2 PM–11 PM, Half day) — start, end, meal break (under 60 minutes counts as paid time), an optional flexible start, and *compressed workweek* for agreed long days (up to 12 hours, no overtime pay within the shift). **Weekly roster**: who works which shift each day; click a day to change just that date (swap a shift, move a rest day). Days already paid are locked; earlier days keep the schedule they had.
2. **Time In / Out** → key in attendance (daily sheet for the team, or per-employee for a period). **Fill** pre-loads scheduled days.
   The **Report** tab shows a period's attendance — totals per employee and each one's daily time record (DTR), with the hours, overtime, lateness and absences payroll will use — and **Download**s it as a PDF (to print and sign) or CSV. A pay run links to the report for its period.
3. **Leaves** → approve/reject leave requests; **Time In / Out → Correction requests** → review employees' missing-time reports (red counts show what's pending).
4. **Pay Runs** → **New pay run** (frequency + period) → add adjustments if any → **Compute draft** → review → **Finalize**.
   Under **Cash advances & loans this payroll**, the pencil sets what this payroll deducts for an advance or loan — the amount you agreed with the employee for this cutoff, or ₱0 to skip it — then recompute. The next payroll goes back to the usual installment.
   Finalizing publishes payslips to employees and locks the period's attendance. **Reopen** if you need to fix something (audit-logged), then recompute and finalize again.
5. Download the payroll register or cash payout sheet from the run. For payslips, tick employees (or **Select all**) → **Download payslips** → one PDF to print, or a ZIP with one file per employee.
   Before finalizing, read the review warnings: a workday with no time in/out and no leave is charged as an absence.
6. **Cash Advances** → **Record advance** when you release one (or **Requests** → **Approve…** an employee's request): amount, deduction per payroll — or **No fixed installment** when you agree on the amount each payroll (set it in each draft; nothing is deducted until you do) — release and first-deduction dates. Print the **agreement**, have the employee sign it, and tick *Signed by the employee on* — an advance can't be saved without it. Each finalized payroll deducts the installment (never more than the net pay); the payroll that includes a separation takes the rest (a paused advance shows up in that payroll's review warnings instead). **Details** shows the history; record a **repayment** made outside payroll there, pause deductions, or change the installment.
   Things payroll now handles by the rules (Oct 2026 review, updated Oct 26 per the owner's compliance spec):
   - **Client Settings → Tax and labor rules** holds the client's choices: taxing a minimum wage earner's extra 30% for a regular holiday worked on the rest day (on), paid vacation leave days a year, taxing SIL converted to cash (on), counting the 13th month as SSS pay (on), a DOLE exemption from the maternity salary differential (reference + valid until), the holiday absence rule (off), and the small retail/service holiday pay exemption.
   - **Holidays** — an unworked regular holiday is paid. Only with the client's *holiday absence rule* on does an unpaid absence on the workday before cost it; the review warnings then name it, so you can enter missing time first.
   - **Minimum wage earners** — rest-day and special-day premiums are taxable; the minimum wage, holiday pay, overtime, night differential stay exempt, and hazard pay only with the hazardous-assignment certification on the employee's **Pay** tab.
   - **Withholding** — a bonus, commission or overtime is taxed at the rate of the regular pay's bracket; for low earners, big one-time payments, or someone with an earlier employer this year, payroll switches to the cumulative average method for the rest of the year (the payslip's notes say which).
   - **SSS** is on everything the month paid — overtime, allowances, de minimis, bonuses, the 13th month — except a Christmas bonus and allowances subject to liquidation (tick them when you add them). The month's last payroll settles it and refunds any over-deduction.
   - **De minimis** — when you add a de minimis allowance or adjustment, pick which benefit it is; the BIR ceiling applies (counting earlier payrolls) and the excess is taxed. For unused leave converted to cash use *Unused leave converted to cash* with the number of days.
   - **Year-end tax adjustment** — the switch appears only for a payroll paid in December; turn it on for the last one of the year. A separating employee's tax is settled on the payroll that includes the last day. If the tax owed is more than that pay, payroll takes what the pay allows, the company pays the rest, and it shows under **Cash Advances → Advanced in payroll** to collect.
   - **Maternity leave** is not paid as salary: add the **SSS maternity benefit** (advance it in full within 30 days of the leave application) and the **salary differential (before employee contributions)** as adjustments. Every month of the leave still pays the usual SSS, PhilHealth and Pag-IBIG; the payroll paying the maternity pay takes the employee's shares for all the leave months.
   - **Deductions** for a company loan, HMO or anything else the law doesn't provide for need the date the employee signed a written authorization (employee's **Pay** tab, or the adjustment form for a one-time *Other* deduction).
   - **PhilHealth** — for a solo parent or a PWD, set *PhilHealth employee half* on the **Pay** tab with the first month and the ID: the government pays the employee's half (shown on the payslip as *NG share – non-collectible*) until the month the ID expires.
7. **BIR & Reports** → **Insights**: pick a report (payroll cost, overtime, attendance, headcount & turnover, leave, cash advances & loans, or a custom report of any payslip figures), the period, department and grouping; read **What stands out**, switch any chart to a table, and download **CSV** or **PDF**. The page link keeps the settings, so a report can be bookmarked or sent to a co-admin. The other tabs: 1601-C, 1604-C alphalist, 2316 (print one or all; BIR copies need the employee's signature and go in as scanned copies), the Annex F list for substituted filing, SSS / PhilHealth / Pag-IBIG reports, **Pay changes** (the year's raises and other rate changes, with the monthly cost), and **Due dates** — this year's filing and payment deadlines (change a date when an agency extends it).
8. **Announcements** → post notices to employees.

## Fixing a mistake after payday
- **Not paid out yet** → open the run → **Reopen as draft** → fix it → recompute → finalize. A reopen doesn't recompute later payslips: if later payrolls of the same people are finalized, reopen those first (latest first) and finalize again oldest first.
- **Underpaid** (missed overtime, a day docked by mistake) → next pay run → **Add adjustment** → *Earning* with the matching classification (e.g. Overtime, Basic pay) and a label naming the period. It is taxed, and counts toward the 13th month, like the original pay.
- **Overpaid** → reopen the paid run and correct it so the taxable pay is right, then on the employee's **Pay** tab add a deduction (*Other*, amount per payroll, balance = the excess) — only with the employee's written consent. A one-time deduction alone would leave the excess in taxable pay.
- **Tax withheld wrong, pay right** → fix the cause (the employee's minimum-wage flag or previous-employer figures, an earning's classification) for future payrolls. The last payroll paid in December with **Year-end tax adjustment** on (or the payroll that includes a separation) then withholds what is short or refunds what is over. **BIR & Reports → Alphalist** lists who is under- or over-withheld. Tax the company failed to withhold is the company's own liability as withholding agent, with penalties, so settle it within the year. If the *taxable amount* was wrong (an earning classified non-taxable, the minimum-wage flag), annualization can't see it: reopen and recompute the payrolls involved.
- **That month's 1601-C is already filed** → report the difference on a later 1601-C (adjustment of taxes withheld from previous months; record it under **1601-C remittances → Adjustment**) or amend the return. Interest and penalties may apply, so check with your accountant.
- **The year's 2316s or alphalist are already out** → after correcting the payrolls, give the employee an amended BIR Form 2316 and file an amended 1604-C alphalist.

## Employees
- **Dashboard** — latest take-home pay, deductions, leave balances.
- **Payslips** — view/download finalized payslips.
- **Attendance** — check records; **report a missing time entry** if something's wrong (HR fixes it).
- **Leaves** — file leave requests. Service Incentive Leave becomes available after 12 months of service, Solo Parent Leave and Special Leave for Women after 6 (the date shows on the card). Maternity leave counts calendar days.
- **Cash Advances** — request an advance (amount and how much to deduct per payroll), see what you owe, what the next payroll deducts (or that the amount is agreed each payroll), and every deduction so far. Advances carry no interest; you sign an authorization before the money is released.
- Lost your code? Ask HR for a new one.
